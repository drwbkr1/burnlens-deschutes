BurnLens · experimental CV-to-GEOINT

Evidence wins. The model doesn’t.

A bounded U-Net trains, evaluates, and reproduces—then loses to RBR. BurnLens rejects the model and carries the stronger baseline into one georeferenced Ward Creek evidence workflow.

Not official wildfire information. Not emergency guidance. Not evacuation, routing, tactical, or incident-command support. Official sources govern.

The promise

A visible chain from evidence to map.

  1. Controlled Sentinel-2 and official-program evidence.
  2. Owner-confirmed prototype regions with explicit unknown rings.
  3. Pre-locked whole-event split and independently rebuilt dataset.
  4. Preregistered RBR plus one bounded CPU U-Net.
  5. Reject the model; retain the stronger baseline.
  6. Write georeferenced rasters, vectors, observations, and package.
  7. Test recovery, reconstruction, rendering, rollback, and known issues.
