How the evidence moves

A gated chain, not a black box.

  1. Official and optical evidence enter source, terms, custody, quality, and uncertainty gates.
  2. Owner-confirmed prototype regions retain explicit unknown rings; owner yes is necessary, never sufficient.
  3. Twelve native-grid patches are divided by a pre-locked 2/2/2 whole-event split and independently reconstructed.
  4. Preregistered RBR and one bounded 117,473-parameter CPU U-Net are evaluated under separate sealed openings.
  5. The U-Net loses and remains diagnostic; RBR becomes the accepted bounded route.
  6. Ward Creek output becomes rasters, vectors, context, observations, an offline interface, and an immutable run package.
  7. Phase Five tests recovery, assurance, reconstruction, packaging, rollback, and known issues without changing the analysis.
Interpretation boundary

What BurnLens does not prove.

No independent truth

Owner-approved prototype regions are not independent ground truth or field validation.

No generalization

Twelve balanced patches, 287 selected cores, and 89 selected test cores do not estimate natural prevalence or complete-scar performance.

No model superiority

The U-Net is not an accepted perimeter, area estimator, or calibrated confidence product. It did not outperform RBR.

No operational claim

The interface is local and offline. BurnLens is not official, deployed, endorsed, field-validated, operational, or emergency-ready.

Two visible medium reliability findings

The Phase Five candidate retains a bounded setuptools advisory under the ZIP-only route and an omitted historical builder identity needed only for exact v0.54 wheel reconstruction. Both include impact and workaround.
